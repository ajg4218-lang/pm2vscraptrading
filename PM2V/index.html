How PM2V Works

A quick guide to how the PM2V Scrap Trading system functions — from getting access to managing inventory, recording transactions, and reading reports.

1Sign InApproved account? Enter your email and password to open your workspace.
2Manage InventoryAdd and update scrap items with category, weight, and stock status.
3Record TransactionsLog every purchase or sale to keep a complete record.
4Review ReportsCheck totals and activity over time; the Owner manages accounts.

PM2V is the shop’s web-based inventory and transaction system. It tracks every scrap item — name, category, weight in kg, and stock status — records each purchase or sale as a transaction, and turns that data into reports so you always know what is in stock, what is moving, and what needs restocking.

The daily flow follows the sidebar from top to bottom:

  • Dashboard — your starting overview: total items, categories, active items, and low-stock warnings.
  • Physical Inventory — add and update every scrap item (category, weight, status).
  • Transactions — record each scrap purchase or sale so nothing goes untracked.
  • Reports — review inventory totals and activity over time.
  • User Management (Owner only) — approve access requests and manage accounts.

Accounts are created through owner approval. Click “Apply for Access.” under the Sign In form, submit your full name, email, and role, then wait. Once the owner approves your application, you can sign in with your email and password.

In Stock — normal levels. Low Stock — at or below the restock point; the Dashboard flags it so you can buy soon. Overstock — above the target; prioritize selling before buying more.

Owner — full control: inventory, transactions, and reports, plus User Management (approving access requests, assigning roles, managing accounts). Secretary — inventory, transactions, and reports. Staff — dashboard, physical inventory, and profile. Your sidebar only shows the pages your role can open.

First, confirm the owner has approved your account — pending applications cannot sign in yet. Already approved? Use “Forgot password?” to reset it via your email, or ask the owner to check your account under User Management.

Open Profile from the sidebar. You can update your display name and change your password there; changes apply to your session right away.
