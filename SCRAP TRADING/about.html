About PM2V Scrap Trading

A trusted partner in sustainable waste management since 2007

Our Story

PM2V Scrap Trading was founded in 2007 in Los Baños, Laguna with a simple mission: to transform waste into value. What started as a small-scale scrap collection operation has grown into a trusted name in sustainable waste management across the region.

For over 17 years, we have been partnering with businesses, institutions, and communities to responsibly collect, sort, and process recyclable materials — keeping valuable resources out of landfills and putting them back into the supply chain.

What We Do

We specialize in the collection, segregation, and trading of recyclable scrap materials including:

  • Metal — Ferrous and non-ferrous metals from industrial and household sources
  • Wood — Pallets (palochina, good lumber), firewood, and reusable timber
  • Plastic — Plastic pallets, containers, and recyclable plastic waste
  • Paper & Carton — Cardboard boxes, office paper, and packaging materials
  • Fiber — Sako (jute sacks) and other fiber-based materials

Our Inventory System

This web-based Inventory Management System was developed to modernize our operations. It allows our team to:

  • Track real-time stock levels of all scrap materials
  • Record transactions and generate accurate reports
  • Manage staff roles with role-based access control
  • Monitor inventory conditions and receive low-stock alerts

The system is designed for use by our Owner, Secretary, and Staff members, each with appropriate permissions to ensure data integrity and security.
