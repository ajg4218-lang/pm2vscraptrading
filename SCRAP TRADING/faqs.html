General Questions

PM2V Scrap Trading is a waste management and scrap trading company based in Los Baños, Laguna. We collect, segregate, and trade recyclable materials including metal, wood, plastic, paper, and fiber since 2007.

We accept five main categories of scrap materials: Metal (ferrous and non-ferrous), Wood (pallets, lumber, firewood), Plastic (pallets, containers), Paper and Carton (cardboard, office paper), and Fiber (jute sacks and similar materials).

We are located in Los Baños, Laguna, Philippines. Our operating hours are Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Account & Access

Click "Apply for Access" on the login page. Fill in your name, email, and desired role. Your application will be reviewed by the system owner, and you will receive access once approved.

Owner — Full access including user management, reports, transactions, and all inventory operations.
Secretary — Can manage transactions, generate reports, and handle inventory records.
Staff — Can manage daily collection logs and view physical inventory.

Account approval is handled by the system owner and typically takes 1–2 business days. You will be able to log in once your application has been approved.

On the login page, enter your email address in the email field, then click "Forgot password?" A password reset link will be sent to your email. Follow the instructions in the email to set a new password.

For security purposes, your account is temporarily locked for 2 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. This protects your account from unauthorized access. Wait for the lockout period to pass, then try again with the correct credentials.

Inventory & System

All inventory is tracked in real-time using our cloud-based system. Each item is recorded with its name, category, weight (in kg), condition, and last update timestamp. Changes are reflected immediately across all user dashboards.

Yes. Owners and Secretaries can generate PDF reports filtered by date range (Today, Weekly, Monthly, or All Time). Reports include inventory summaries, category breakdowns, condition status, recent activity, and transaction history.

Yes. We use Firebase Authentication for secure login, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and Firestore security rules that enforce role-based access control. Only authorized users can view or modify data relevant to their role.

The system automatically flags items below the low-stock threshold (5 kg) and displays alerts on the Reports page. This helps the team prioritize collection and restocking efforts.
